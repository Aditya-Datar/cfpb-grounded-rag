Takeaways and limitations

Fix retrieval first, then let a validated checker gate the draft

1 · Where to invest

Retrieval was the bottleneck (gap +70 points). After reranking it is +11; most remaining errors happen with the correct passage, in generation.

2 · The checker is the lever

Checking and rewriting flagged sentences adds 25 points. Prompt changes add nothing once it is on.

3 · Where this lands

75% acceptable drafts. Magesh et al. report 65% accuracy for Lexis+ AI on a different task with lawyer judges: same range, not a head-to-head.

Limitations

  • The same checker drives the rewrites and scores them; a human check of 24 rewritten sentences is in progress.
  • Rewriting makes drafts address the complaint less often (48% → 34%).
  • 65 held-out complaints: wide CIs. 100 of 150 checker labels are AI-assisted.
  • Best reranker is a hosted GPT model.
  • Drafts assist a compliance officer; they are not legal advice.
