MidtermFinal (held-out)

Held-out: 65 complaints; checker: 100 labelled pairs. The midterm accuracy used the lenient 0.5 checker, which flatters it. EXPERIMENT_LOG H1–H5, E12, E17.

~55 seconds. Left: the plan we showed at the midterm, and every item is done. We added dense and hybrid retrieval and then an LLM reranker; ran the attribution experiment on all 127 complaints and once on an untouched held-out half; the chunking ablation shows chunks cut at section boundaries beat fixed 200-word windows by about 15 points hit@5; the detector for complaints no regulation in our corpus covers went from AUROC 0.43 to 0.74; and the demo became a full officer web app. Three changes of direction, each for a reason: the NLI checker only reached 0.70 against our labels, so we paired it with an LLM judge; once retrieval improved, generation became the bottleneck, so we rewrite flagged sentences; and credit-bureau duties are in the FCRA statute, not Reg V, so we added it. Right: the four headline numbers, midterm to final. Retrieval top-1 from 6 to 77 percent; checker agreement from 61 to 83; fully correct sentences from 18 to 69; and the gap caused by retrieval from 70 points down to 11.
