Same drafter (Nemotron Super), checker and rewriting on both sides; gap = oracle − retrieved, paired bootstrap (per complaint); bars are per sentence. Midterm row: all 127 complaints. EXPERIMENT_LOG H2, E13, E16, E17.

~75 seconds, the main slide. Each bar is all the sentences our drafts produced. Blue is correct: supported and citing the right section. Orange is what retrieval costs: the gap between drafting from the retrieved passage and from the correct one. Purple is what is still wrong even with the correct passage, so it is generation's fault. At the midterm, with BM25, the orange part was 70 points: retrieval was the problem. With the final retriever it shrinks to 7 points before rewriting, not significant, and generation is now the big purple block. So we check every sentence and rewrite the flagged ones from the passage: correct sentences go from 44 to 69 percent, a 25-point gain, and the retrieval gap is 11 points, CI 0.00 to 0.23. Ablations: if the NLI checker alone decides what to rewrite, we only reach 0.56, so the validated checker is what makes rewriting work. A prompt telling the drafter to quote the provision helps by 17 points alone but adds nothing on top of rewriting. Error analysis: 77 percent of sentences cite the gold section, 4 an accepted equivalent, 7 the wrong section of the right law and 12 a different law. If asked why the correct passage still is not 100 percent: the drafter applies the rule to the complaint's facts or drops a condition, which the passage alone cannot support.
