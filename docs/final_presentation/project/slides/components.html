~60 seconds. Setup, top row: public CFPB complaints and eCFR regulations plus the FCRA statute; 198 complaints labelled by two different LLMs under our rubric, every disagreement resolved by a team member, kappa 0.85 to 0.90; every design choice was made on a 62-complaint dev half, and the 65-complaint held-out half was run once per pre-registered rule. Baselines: BM25 for retrieval, the NLI checker alone as in SummaC, and the correct passage as the upper bound. Our main metric is strict: a sentence counts only if the passage supports it AND it cites the right section, which is the legal definition from Magesh et al. Left chart: BM25, the midterm retriever, found the right section first only 6 percent of the time. Hybrid search, a prior toward the classifier's regulation, and an LLM reranker that reads the complaint took it to 54, and GPT with our labelling rubric to 77. Right chart: the SummaC-style NLI checker reached only 70 percent balanced accuracy on regulation text; averaging it with the Nemotron Ultra judge reached 83, a significant 15-point gain. Specialised checkers such as MiniCheck, HHEM and AlignScore did not beat the pair.
